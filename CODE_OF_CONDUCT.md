# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute without being
harassed or dismissed. We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of
experience level, background, identity or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and specific in reviews: point at code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before rewriting someone's contribution.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change for scope reasons.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues or pull requests.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who
violate this code. Report problems privately to the maintainer; reports are
handled confidentially.
